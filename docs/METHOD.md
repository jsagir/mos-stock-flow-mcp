# `/m:os` method basis

This is a **PWS-informed proposed adaptation**, not a canonical or field-validated PWS procedure. PWS Book Week 6, “Systems Problems,” sequences causal-loop mapping, stock-and-flow/delay mapping, nested levels, then leverage-point analysis. The combined toolbox record describes its procedure as proposed and unvalidated.

1. State purpose and boundary, affected actors, evidence, and contested assumptions.
2. Map causal influences; label polarity and evidence. An arrow is a claim to examine, not proof.
3. Find accumulations (stocks), inflows/outflows (rates), and delays. Do not invent numbers or mistake a rate for a stock.
4. Map containing and component levels. Record who draws the boundary and what may be outside it.
5. Compare leverage hypotheses across parameters, information, rules, goals, and assumptions. Record uncertainty and who might be affected.

Wicked problems can have disputed scope and no final stopping rule. A map is a diagnostic artifact for inquiry, not a causal finding or a decision about stakeholder values.

Source basis: PWS Book, Week 6, “Systems Problems,” and “Decomplexifying.” The source work is not a public procedure specification. This service presents the sequence above as its own proposed adaptation and labels the combined toolbox procedure unvalidated.
