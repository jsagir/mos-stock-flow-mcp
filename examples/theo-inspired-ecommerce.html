Possible system explanation

For this fictional shop, the map proposes a simple customer path: campaign exposure builds familiarity, some familiar people visit, some visitors buy, and some customers buy again. Each transition is a hypothesis; this is a stage-transition sketch, not a proven causal explanation.

What to look for next: Compare dated campaign, visit, first-order, and repeat-order records. Find cases where one stage increased without the next, then check what differed.

How to read this map

  1. Boxes are stocks: amounts that build up or run down over time.
  2. Pipe arrows are flows: what enters, leaves, or moves between stocks. A valve marks the rate of change.
  3. Dashed arrows are influences: a proposed relationship that may change a flow. “+” means both move in the same direction; “−” means they move in opposite directions.
  • Solid pipe stock changes
  • Dashed arrow possible influence
  • + / − / ? influence direction
