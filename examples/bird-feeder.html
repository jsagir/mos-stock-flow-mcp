The bird feeder

A person adds a bird feeder to make breakfast more pleasant. The lecture traces the reinforcing loop—and then follows the side effects back to the original frustration.

  6. More birds outside
  7. More pleasant breakfasts
Reinforcing causal loop+ Both variables move in the same direction

Then follow the side effects

The PWS chapter traces how more birds at the feeder can bring back frustration through several routes:

Seed and money

More birds→+More feed→+More trips to the store→+More money worries→+More frustration

Squirrels

Squirrels arrive→−Birds get chased off→Less birdsong at breakfast

Spilled feed

Squirrels spill feed→+More mess and weeds→+More cleanup work→+More frustration

Patio cleanup

More birds→+Bird poop on patio→+More cleanup→+More frustration
Have I really reduced my frustration?

The diagram makes the intended benefit and the unintended costs visible together. Their real size and timing would need evidence.
