    R means reinforcing; B means balancing. These labels follow the signs on your arrows. They classify the map’s logic, not proven real-world effects. The scan is bounded; dense maps may omit some cycles.

    Systems thinking guide
    1. Start with purpose. Whose situation are you mapping, and what are you trying to understand?
    2. Look for a pattern. Compare concrete cases, including one that does not fit.
    3. Map influences. Add variables, then connect a cause to an effect. Use + when both move in the same direction and − when they move in opposite directions.
    4. Find accumulations. Add stocks and the rates that fill or drain them. Keep unknown values unknown.
    5. Explore a possible change. Play only with stated values and rates; the result is a model behavior, not proof about the real system.
